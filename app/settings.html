Gemini Speech to Text

Hold your hotkey, talk, let go: the text is typed where your cursor is.

Get a free key at aistudio.google.com/apikey. It's stored encrypted in your system keychain, never in a plain file.
Click Record, then press and release the keys you want to hold (Esc cancels). Or type them joined with +: CTRL, SUPER (WIN), ALT and SHIFT match either side, e.g. CTRL+SUPER or KEY_RIGHTCTRL.
System default follows the input chosen in your system's sound settings, checked each time you start dictating, so switching or plugging in a mic needs no restart. If a chosen mic is missing, the default is used and you get a notification.
