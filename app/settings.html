Gemini Speech to Text

Hold your hotkey, talk, let go: the text is typed where your cursor is.

Get a free key at aistudio.google.com/apikey. It's stored encrypted in your system keychain, never in a plain file.
Keys to hold, joined with +. CTRL, SUPER (WIN), ALT and SHIFT match either side. Example: CTRL+SUPER, or KEY_RIGHTCTRL.
Recordings quieter than this aren't sent. Lower it (e.g. -48) if quiet speech gets ignored; raise it if background noise turns into words.
